About Our School

An English medium school with over 15 years of excellence in education, serving students from Kindergarten to Grade 10. Founded in 2006, our school is dedicated to fostering holistic development, paying close attention to academics, sports, and extracurricular activities. Centrally located on the main road in Ara, Bhojpur, just a 5-minute walk from the Ara Railway Station, we offer convenient transportation services to ensure easy access for all students.
